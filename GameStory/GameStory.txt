Game Name:Demonic Invasion

========================================================================================================================================================

Characters
The Traveler (Player):A Hunter who travels across the world of Ivory, fighting demons and absorbing fragments of their spiritual power.

The Butler:A mysterious butler who assists the player.
			Though he appears calm and refined, he is actually an entity beyond comprehension, something ancient and unknowable that exists beyond the understanding of mortals.

The Stable Master:A non-playable character who takes care of the stables.
			He manages the horses and transport within the fortress city and often speaks about the history of the world, sharing knowledge of past kingdoms and the events that led to the demonic invasion.

Lilith:The Demon Queen and creator of the demonic hordes invading Ivory.(Final Boss of Version 2)

Lucifer:The ruler of the abyss who manipulates events from beyond the world and stands behind the invasion. (Final Boss of Version 1)

Back Story of the World in Demonic Invasion:

Long ago, in the realm known as Ivory, the world was a place of peace and prosperity.
Humans, elves, and dwarves lived side by side, under the protection of both magic and steel.
The gods themselves smiled upon this land, and all seemed well.

But the peace would not last.
One fateful night, the heavens themselves broke open, and falling stars rained down upon the earth.

Massive craters, known as Sanctuaries, were carved into the land, their deep scars burning with an unnatural, malevolent energy.

At first, the kingdoms believed the stars to be a gift from the gods, a divine blessing that would bring new power to the world.

But the truth was far darker.

Years later, the Sanctuaries split open—and from within emerged demons, twisted beings of destruction, their eyes burning with rage.

The creatures were immune to mortal weapons and capable of terrible feats of magic.

Entire kingdoms fell in a matter of days, their armies slaughtered and cities reduced to ash.

With the rise of the Demon Queen, Lilith, the true scope of the calamity became clear.

The demons were not mindless beasts—they were led by a being of intelligence and malice, a force that had set this chain of destruction in motion.

Lilith, the demons' mother and creator, sought to reshape the world in her image.

The remnants of humanity, elves, and dwarves fled to the safety of massive fortress cities, built to withstand the onslaught of Lilith’s forces.

But even behind their walls, they were not safe.

The demons grew in number and power, slowly creeping across the land.

Hope came in an unexpected form.

It was discovered that the fragments left behind by fallen demons could be used by mortals.

These fragments contained spiritual power, granting those who wielded them the strength to fight back.

These warriors, known as Hunters, were the last hope for the world.

They hunted demons, absorbing the fragments they left behind, growing stronger with each battle.

Yet, even as the Hunters rose to challenge the darkness, a greater threat loomed.

The fragments were not without their price.

Some Hunters fell to corruption, becoming twisted reflections of the very demons they fought.

The more they absorbed, the closer they came to becoming the monsters they sought to destroy.

As the world teetered on the edge of annihilation, a new generation of Hunters appeared.

You, the Traveler, are among them.

Story of Demonic Invasion:

Long ago, in the realm known as Ivory, the world was a place of peace and prosperity. Humans, elves, and dwarves lived side by side beneath open skies, their kingdoms flourishing under the protection of both magic and steel. The gods themselves seemed to smile upon this land, and for generations, the people believed their harmony would last forever.

But peace is a fragile thing.

One fateful night, the heavens broke open.

The sky tore apart in blinding light as falling stars rained down upon the world. Each star struck the earth with catastrophic force, carving enormous craters into the land. These scars would later be known as Sanctuaries—vast pits of twisted stone that pulsed with an unnatural, malevolent energy.

At first, the kingdoms believed the stars were a gift from the gods, a blessing meant to bring new power and knowledge. Scholars, kings, and priests alike sent expeditions to study the Sanctuaries.

But the truth was far darker.

Years passed.

Then one day, the Sanctuaries split open.

From within them emerged creatures unlike anything the world had ever seen—demons, twisted beings born of darkness and rage. Their eyes burned with hatred, their bodies warped by unnatural power. Mortal weapons barely harmed them, and their magic shattered armies like glass.

Entire kingdoms fell within days. Cities burned. Nations collapsed.

Hope seemed lost.

Then the truth behind the invasion was revealed.

At the center of the catastrophe stood Lilith, the Demon Queen—the mother and creator of the demonic hordes. Unlike the monsters she commanded, Lilith possessed terrible intelligence and purpose. The Sanctuaries were not accidents.

They were invasions.

Lilith sought to reshape the world of Ivory in her image, turning the living world into a realm ruled by demons.

The surviving races—humans, elves, and dwarves—retreated into enormous fortress cities, desperate strongholds built to withstand the endless waves of demonic attacks. But even behind stone walls and iron gates, they were not safe.

The demons multiplied.

Their influence spread across the land like a plague.

Ivory began to fall.

Then, unexpectedly, hope emerged.

Hunters discovered that when demons were slain, they left behind fragments of strange energy—crystallized remnants of their spiritual power. These fragments could be absorbed by mortals, granting incredible strength, supernatural abilities, and resilience.

Those who learned to wield this power became known as Hunters.

Warriors who hunted demons, absorbed their fragments, and grew stronger with each battle.

But power always carries a cost.

The fragments were not pure.

Some Hunters absorbed too much of the demonic energy and began to change. Their minds twisted. Their bodies warped. Slowly, they became reflections of the very monsters they hunted.

Corruption spread among the Hunters themselves.

As the world teetered on the edge of annihilation, a new generation of Hunters rose to challenge the darkness.

Among them… you.

You are not a native of Ivory.

You are a traveler between worlds, a wanderer who has crossed countless realms in search of power, purpose, and truth. Each world you visit leaves its mark upon you, shaping you into something greater—and something more dangerous.

In Ivory, you walk the path of the Hunter.

Across the ruined lands, you will travel through forgotten cities, corrupted forests, shattered kingdoms, and the burning edges of the Sanctuaries themselves. Along the way you will complete dangerous quests, solve ancient puzzles left behind by lost civilizations, and uncover the buried history of the world.

Some Hunters you meet will become allies.

Others will see you as a rival.

And some may already be too far consumed by corruption to be saved.

With every demon you defeat, you will absorb their fragments, growing stronger… but also drawing closer to the edge of corruption yourself.

Yet even Lilith is not the true master of this calamity.

Hidden beyond the veil of the Sanctuaries, behind the demonic invasion and the fall of Ivory, lies a far greater power—the ancient ruler of the abyss.

Lucifer.

The fallen king who manipulates the darkness from beyond the world.

Lilith is his queen.

The demons are his army.

And the Sanctuaries are merely the gates.

Your journey will take you across the broken world of Ivory as you hunt demons, gather power, uncover forgotten truths, and confront the forces that seek to devour this realm.

But the path you walk will shape your fate.

Will you master the fragments and become powerful enough to challenge the abyss itself?

Or will the demonic power within you eventually consume you?

At the end of your journey awaits the final battle—deep within the heart of the Sanctuaries, where the boundary between worlds is thin.

There, you will face Lucifer, the architect of the invasion.

The fate of Ivory will be decided by your actions.

Whether the world is saved…

or consumed…

will depend entirely on you.
